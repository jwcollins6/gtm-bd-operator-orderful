# ICP — [Client name]

Company-level fit criteria — who should get targeted, not who at that company
gets talked to (that's `persona-template.md`). Built from real evidence, not
assumed or taken from the client's own description alone.

## How to build this

Not a fixed checklist — these are the usual inputs, but which ones actually
apply, and in what order, can differ by client.

1. **Review of current customers** — see
   `company-understanding-inventory.md`'s "Current customers" section. Look
   specifically for fit patterns across the base (size, industry, deal size,
   buyer role), not just who they are.
2. **Review of recently won deals** — distinct from the full customer base:
   what's actually working right now, which can differ from the base if
   positioning has shifted since older customers were won.
3. **Review of inbound leads** — see `company-understanding-inventory.md`'s
   "Recent inbound leads" section. Who's self-selecting in, and whether that
   matches or diverges from who actually converts.
4. **The client's own stated understanding** — their own description of who
   they sell to. One input, not the whole picture — checked against the
   evidence above, not taken at face value (see `../docs/OFFERING_SPEC.md`).
5. **Other client-specific sources** — may differ by client; note whatever else
   actually applied here (win/loss analysis, sales team interviews, market
   research, etc.).
