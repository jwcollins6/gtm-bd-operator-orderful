# Proof points / case studies — Orderful

**Status: research-derived concept, not client-verified.** Built 2026-09-17
from Orderful's own published case studies and testimonials (homepage,
pricing page). Same caveat as `company-understanding-inventory.md`'s
"Current customers" section: these are Orderful's own marketing-selected
highlights, not confirmed as the strongest or most representative stories —
real deal data would confirm or replace them. This is the single source for
proof points used across `../templates/outreach-templates.md` — templates
reference this file rather than duplicating quotes inline, so updating a
stat or swapping a proof point happens in one place.

## By persona

### Ops/Systems lead at a consumer brand entering retail

**Liquid Death** — Derek Wu, Business Applications Manager
> "With Orderful, we have visibility and control over our EDI. It's great to
> finally own the keys to the kingdom and not rely on outside parties."

70% reduction in EDI costs · 80% reduction in time to set up trading
partners · 90% faster error resolution.
Source: [Liquid Death case study](https://www.orderful.com/resources/case-studies/liquid-death)
**Used in:** Template 1 (`outreach-templates.md`). Fits this persona because
it's the same company shape as True Classic and other DTC-to-retail brands —
a small ops/business-systems function owning EDI, not a big IT department.

### IT/EDI technical owner at a manufacturer or larger enterprise

**NFI** — David Broering, President of Integrated Logistics
> "With Orderful, we routinely set up new partners within less than five
> days. Much of that is because so many partners are already in Orderful's
> network, which makes it easy to establish new connections."

<5 days to onboard new trading partners · 90% reduction in onboarding time
· 30% faster time to market with EDI trading partners.
Source: [NFI case study](https://www.orderful.com/resources/case-studies/nfi)
**Used in:** Template 3. The network-effect explanation is the technical
credibility angle this persona responds to.

**Grosfillex North America** — Jim Berger, IT Manager (alternate option, not
currently used in any template)
> "Orderful has helped Grosfillex drive an 83% reduction in customer
> onboarding time. We decreased onboarding time from six months to one
> month, and is confident it can achieve further reductions."

100% reduction in partner customizations · 83% reduction in trading partner
setup time · 28% reduction in EDI costs overall.
Source: testimonial on Orderful's pricing page (no standalone case-study
page found). Another IT-Manager-led story for the same persona as NFI —
worth testing as an alternate if NFI's proof point underperforms.

### Owner/executive at a smaller logistics, carrier, or 3PL company

**KBX Technology Solutions** — Jim Bruckert, EDI Team Leader
> "We have reduced onboarding time by 50% with Orderful. If we're not
> moving loads, we're not making money. Orderful gives us the real-time data
> and notifications we need to keep everything moving."

100% partner self-service onboarding · 50% decrease in time to onboard ·
70% reduction in EDI onboarding efforts.
Source: [KBX case study](https://www.orderful.com/resources/case-studies/kbx)
**Used in:** Template 4. The revenue-framed language ("if we're not moving
loads, we're not making money") matches this persona's own priorities.

**Heartland Logistics Group** — John Kuhlmann, President (alternate option
for Template 4)
> "With Orderful, we're up and running — and generating revenue — within a
> week. If we didn't have Orderful, we would probably have half the number
> of clients we have today."

5 days to onboard new customer · 100% growth in client base · zero
additional headcount.
Source: testimonial on Orderful's pricing page. The strongest headcount-
efficiency proof point in this set — worth leading with for a prospect
where "grow without adding people" is likely to land harder than raw speed.

## Open gaps

- All five are Orderful's own marketing-selected highlights, not verified as
  representative — real deal data would confirm, replace, or reweight these.
- No proof point exists for the "SaaS & Tech Platforms" persona, consistent
  with that persona itself being unbuilt (see `persona-template.md`'s Open
  Gaps).
- Caraway and True Classic — both used elsewhere in this repo as validating
  examples for the signal inventory — aren't included here since neither has
  a full public case study with a quote and stats the way these five do;
  Caraway is a customer logo and a press story, not a published case study.
- Grosfillex's and Heartland's sources are pricing-page testimonials, not
  standalone case-study pages — worth confirming those testimonials are
  still current before using them in front of anyone.
