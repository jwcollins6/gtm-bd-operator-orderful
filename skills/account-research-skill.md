# Account research — skill

How to actually do the research that fills in
`../templates/account-research-brief.md`. See
`../docs/process/outbound-pipeline.md` stage 6 for where this fits in the
pipeline.

## Process

1. Confirm the signal — read what actually triggered this (see
   `../onboarding/signal-inventory-template.md` for what this client's signals
   mean and how urgent each tier is).
2. Check company basics — pull whatever's available from the source that
   surfaced this (name, size, location, website).
3. Run the ICP fit check — compare against `../onboarding/icp-template.md`; if
   it fails, stop here and note why rather than building out a full brief for a
   non-fit.
4. Map to a persona — use `../onboarding/persona-template.md` to figure out
   which buyer type this contact represents, so drafting later uses the right
   angle.
5. Check for a prior relationship — is this genuinely new, or an account
   already touched (see `../docs/process/inbound-process.md` stage 3 for the
   same check run on the inbound side).
6. Pull recent public activity — anything relevant to the signal that makes
   outreach feel specific rather than generic.
7. Apply this client's research priorities — the client-specific section of
   the brief template. Don't skip it; it's usually the part that makes a brief
   actually useful instead of generic.

## What NOT to do

Never fabricate a finding to fill a brief section — an unconfirmed fact gets
marked as unconfirmed, not guessed at and presented as real (see
`../governance/README.md`).
