# Account research brief — template

The shape of the research a human (or eventually the agent) uses to draft
outreach or evaluate a surfaced account — see
`../docs/process/outbound-pipeline.md` stage 6. Produced fresh per company,
every time a signal fires — not a one-time onboarding artifact like the
templates in `../onboarding/`.

## Universal sections — every brief has these, any client

- **Company basics** — name, size, location, website
- **Signal(s) that triggered this** — what fired, when, from where
- **ICP fit check** — quick pass/fail against `../onboarding/icp-template.md`,
  and why
- **Persona mapping** — which persona (see `../onboarding/persona-template.md`)
  this contact/account maps to. **For a Hot-scored account
  (`../score/scoring-model.md`), map both the primary persona and its paired
  second contact** (per `../onboarding/persona-template.md`'s
  multi-threading section) — not just one. Don't invent a name to fill the
  slot; an unconfirmed second contact gets noted as an enrichment gap, same
  as the primary.
- **Prior relationship check** — is this new, or have we touched this
  account before
- **Recent public activity** — news, expansion, anything relevant to the signal

## Client-specific research priorities

**Status: research-derived concept, not client-verified** (see
`../onboarding/signal-inventory-template.md`'s own status note — same
caveat applies here). What's worth checking on every Orderful account brief,
informed by `../onboarding/icp-template.md`,
`../onboarding/persona-template.md`, and `../onboarding/signal-inventory-template.md`:

- **First EDI relationship, or scaling an existing one?** A brand hitting its
  first EDI-requiring retailer needs a different pitch (Pixel, no-expertise-
  required) than one that's "a major wholesale player" already running EDI
  elsewhere and hitting scale limits (Mosaic, growth-friction angle). Check
  for other existing wholesale/retail relationships beyond the one that
  triggered the signal, not just the triggering one.
- **Does a Tier 1 compliance-policy-change signal also apply?** If the
  triggering signal was a new retailer launch (signal #1), separately check
  whether that same retailer has an active compliance-policy-change signal
  (#2) live right now — see `../onboarding/signal-inventory-template.md`'s
  stacking note in `../score/scoring-model.md`. Two real signals on one
  account is a materially stronger case than one, and changes which outreach
  angle (growth vs. risk) leads.
- **Rough trading-partner-count estimate**, even a loose one (does this look
  like a 1-2 partner company, a dozen, or 20+?) — feeds the ICP size/band
  check in `../onboarding/icp-template.md` and hints at which of Orderful's
  four pricing tiers is realistic, without needing an exact number.
- **Which persona is likely to hold this relationship** — see
  `../onboarding/persona-template.md`. A consumer brand vs. a manufacturer vs.
  a smaller logistics company points to a different title to look for in
  enrichment, not just a different message once found.
